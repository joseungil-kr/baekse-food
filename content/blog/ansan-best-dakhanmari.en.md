<h2 class="h2">The Pinnacle of Chicken Cuisine, Born from 35 Years of Tradition</h2>
<p class="lead">Starting as a Samgyetang (ginseng chicken soup) specialty store, we developed our own unique 'Dakhanmari' sauce after years of extensive research.</p>
<p>Baekse Dakhanmari is more than just a meal; it is the culmination of decades of culinary know-how and deep, rich flavors. With the same dedication we put into our very first bowl of Samgyetang, we now captivate our guests with a perfectly balanced Dakhanmari dish.</p>

<h3 class="h3" style="margin-top:32px">A Deep, Rich Broth Boiled Four Times</h3>
<p>The core of Baekse Dakhanmari lies in its <strong>broth</strong>. We carefully select the freshest ingredients and go through a meticulous process of boiling the broth four times. This secret recipe completely removes any unwanted poultry smell while maximizing a deep, savory umami flavor.</p>

<h3 class="h3" style="margin-top:32px">Handmade Side Dishes and Signature Sauce</h3>
<p>Our dedication goes beyond the main dish. Every side dish and sauce that accompanies the meal is made with care. The kimchi and fresh side dishes prepared in-house, along with our <strong>exclusively developed signature sauce</strong> that elevates the taste of the meat, are the irreplaceable charms of Baekse Dakhanmari.</p>
