<h2 class="h2">Core Checkpoints for a Safe and Reliable Startup</h2>
<p class="lead">Countless restaurants open and close every day. To increase the probability of success, systematic preparation and a verified system are essential. Assess your readiness through the 5 checklists below.</p>

<h3 class="h3" style="margin-top:32px">1. Can you produce consistent taste without culinary expertise?</h3>
<p>A restaurant that relies heavily on a head chef's skills will lose its flavor and regular customers when the chef changes. You must check <strong>if the headquarters provides sauces and core ingredients in One-Pack form.</strong> Baekse Food supplies ready-to-use One-Packs from our direct CK factory, allowing anyone to replicate our 35-year traditional taste.</p>

<h3 class="h3" style="margin-top:32px">2. Is the menu stable regardless of the season?</h3>
<p>Trendy menus that only sell well in the summer or flash in the winter are dangerous. A Korean cuisine-based menu with solid demand throughout all four seasons is ideal. Baekse Food eliminated off-seasons with a hybrid composition covering both lunch (Barley Bibimbap) and dinner (Dakhanmari, Pocha).</p>

<h3 class="h3" style="margin-top:32px">3. Are there unnecessary bubbles (franchise fees, interior, etc.)?</h3>
<p>Starting with excessive loans for initial capital makes it difficult even to cover interest. Choose an honest brand that allows autonomous interior construction and doesn't force royalties or hidden margins.</p>

<h3 class="h3" style="margin-top:32px">4. Can additional revenue be generated through delivery and takeout?</h3>
<p>The era of relying solely on dine-in sales is over. You need to check if the main menu is suitable for delivery and takeout, and if delivery-specialized sub-menus (like Baekse Pocha appetizers) can be structured.</p>

<h3 class="h3" style="margin-top:32px">5. Does the headquarters provide continuous menu development and support?</h3>
<p>Partner with a headquarters that doesn't just abandon you after opening, but actively operates directly-managed stores and consistently provides new menu developments and marketing support aligned with market trends.</p>
